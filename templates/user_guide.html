Your first few minutes

  1. Create your account and verify your email. Follow the verification message before signing in. If it does not arrive, check spam and use the resend option on the sign-in page.
  2. Make your profile yours. Add an avatar, a short bio, and a few interests. You can return to the appearance tools whenever you want.
  3. Write a first post. Use New Post on your profile. Say hello, share something you made, or tell people what you are into. Check the destination and audience before posting.
  4. Find a place to hang out. Browse Vibes or open Circles. You can request membership or accept an invitation to a Circle.
  5. Set your preferences. Review Privacy, Notifications, and Account safety from Account settings.

Standard signup is for people aged 13 and over. Age and account safeguards can limit which people and posts you can interact with.

Your profile and connections

Open Your profile and use its editing controls to update the information and appearance options shown there. Account settings holds your handle, optional codename, avatar, timezone, and Last.fm username.

Profiles support separate day and night appearances, visual theme tools, and a CSS editor for more detailed customization. You do not need to know CSS to start with the visual tools. Save your changes and check both appearances.

Use the friend and follow controls on profiles where available. Friend requests need acceptance; following is a separate connection. Your Top 9 is part of your profile. Connections do not override somebody's privacy, age, or contact restrictions.

Posts, replies, and media

  1. Choose New Post and check where the post will go: your profile, a Vibe, or a Circle.
  2. Add text and any supported attachment. The composer shows the available media options and limits. Add a useful description for an image when that field is available.
  3. Review the audience and any sharing choices, then submit the post.

Posts and replies support Markdown. The Markdown Guide explains formatting such as emphasis, links, and lists.

Use the post's … menu for the actions you are allowed to take, such as editing your own post, viewing edit history, deleting, or reporting. Use Reply or the comment form to join a conversation, and reaction buttons to respond quickly.

Click a gallery image to open it. GIF previews in galleries can remain still until opened. Media settings contains your playback preferences. A gallery only includes images from posts available to you on the page.

Vibes

Vibes are themed spaces for shared interests, feelings, and conversations. Explore Vibes is the directory: search names and descriptions, browse alphabetically, or sort by recent activity visible to you. Each card links to the Vibe and its day and night appearances. Start at Explore Vibes, or follow a Vibe link on a post. Select Vibes in the composer when you want to contribute to those spaces.

A public Vibe does not make every post visible to everyone. Individual post audiences, age restrictions, blocks, account status, and moderation still apply. Vibe managers have separate tools for the Vibes they manage. Managers can publish rules, update the welcome message and appearance, and remove or restore a post within their Vibe without deleting the original. Site owners and admins can archive or restore a Vibe and assign Vibe Managers. An archived Vibe stays out of discovery and cannot receive new posts; existing content keeps its access protections.

Joining and managing Circles

Open Circles to see your memberships and invitations. Read a Circle's description and rules before joining.

  • Invited: open the invitation and choose Accept invitation if you want to join. An invitation alone does not show members-only posts.
  • Request access / Pending: submit a join request and wait for a manager's decision. A pending request is not membership.
  • Member: you can read eligible members-only posts, including earlier posts, and participate. Leaving or removal ends access to private posts and replies.
  • Hidden Circle: it stays out of discovery; you need an appropriate invitation to find and join it.

You can create a Circle from the Circles page. Owners manage the name, description, image, rules, viewing mode, and membership. Owners can appoint moderators and transfer ownership. Moderators help manage members and Circle reports within their permissions.

Managers can pin a welcome post and use the Circle report queue to record a decision and take the available action, including reversible removal. Circle reports and private reports to site staff are separate. Circle name numbers distinguish communities with the same name; they are separate from the random ID in a Circle's link.

Who can see a Circle post?

A Circle's viewing mode and a post's audience are separate choices. Making a Circle public does not publish its older private posts.

Circle viewing choices
Circle and postWhat a nonmember can see
Public Circle + Public postEligible visitors can read the post and view its attachments.
Public Circle + Private to this CircleThe original post and its attachments stay members-only.
Members-only CirclePosts stay members-only. A discoverable Circle may show its introduction; a hidden Circle remains restricted.

Only Public Circles offer the Public post choice. When creating or editing your own post there, choose Public or Private to this Circle. Circle replies, the member list, and edit history remain restricted to authorized members; public reading does not grant participation.

Sharing is an additional choice: a private Circle post can appear in a share outside the Circle if its author enables that permission. See the next section before enabling it.

Sharing and permissions

Use Share where it is offered to share a post, your own posted image, a Circle, or a Vibe. The share dialog offers the available link and Neonrest sharing options. In the Neonrest composer, review the preview, add optional commentary, and submit when ready.

A connected post share can display the original text and attachments with attribution and a link. It refers to the current original: edits, deletion, moderation, and permission changes affect what people can see.

Public posts can be shared subject to the existing access safeguards. For a private Circle post, the original author must enable Allow sharing outside this Circle. This allows eligible nonmembers to see the content and attachments in a share, while the original private post, replies, and member information remain restricted.

Authors can turn that permission off later to hide the shared content from nonmembers on Neonrest. This cannot recall screenshots, copied text, or material someone has saved elsewhere. Sharing a Circle link does not grant membership.

Notifications

Open the mailbox icon to see notifications. Following View marks that item read while keeping its message in your history. You can also use Mark as read.

Inside a Circle, Notify me about new posts opts you into that Circle's new-post notifications. Use Mute to stop those alerts; existing notification history remains. Account notification preferences also apply.

A new-post badge on Circles shows activity since your last visit and clears after you view the Circle. An old notification can remain in history even if the linked content is no longer available to you.

Privacy and safety

Review Privacy in Account settings for the available profile, contact, audience, block, and mute controls. Check individual posting choices too; joining a community does not replace them.

Age safeguards, blocks, membership, account restrictions, and moderation can change what you see. A direct link or a search result never grants access by itself.

Use Report on the relevant post, reply, or profile when it is available. Choose a report shared with Circle managers for a Circle issue, or the site-report option for Neonrest staff. Follow the form's explanation of who receives it.

Common questions and support

Why does a page say “Page unavailable”?

The item may have moved, been removed, or be outside your current access. Check that you are signed into the intended account and still have the required Circle membership. Privacy, age, blocking, or moderation restrictions can also apply.

Why is a Circle or gallery empty?

You may not yet be a member, or there may be no posts or images available to your account. Public Circles only show their public posts to nonmembers. A gallery reflects the visible posts on the current page.

Why can't I edit or manage something?

The controls depend on ownership, your role, and your current access. Some privileged actions require a fresh password and authenticator or recovery-code confirmation. Normal membership does not grant Circle management.

How do I report a bug or ask for help?

Contact Neonrest support. Include the page URL, what you tried, what you expected, and what happened. Do not send passwords, authenticator codes, or recovery codes. The dev log records recent changes.
