Advertising and sponsorships

Limited, clearly labeled advertisements help support Neonrest. Direct sponsors may appear as up to four cards beside Feed and Vibe pages and one lower banner. A page load selects eligible campaigns; there are no timed ad refreshes. Small screens place cards below the timeline. Private Circle pages, profiles and private account screens do not carry these placements.

How selection works

Direct advertisements use the current public Vibe or a general placement, campaign schedule and random rotation. They do not use private content, your contact list or sensitive profile fields. Ad selection does not change chronological post ordering.

Sponsor standards

Every sponsor must be identified in its advertisement or landing page. Staff review the copy, local artwork and destination before activation. Ads must be suitable for a mixed-age audience. We do not accept adult sexual content, gambling, tobacco or vaping, weapons sales, unlawful goods, discriminatory or hateful material, political persuasion campaigns, deceptive claims or downloads, financial schemes, or requests to send private account information.

Ads may not resemble account alerts, navigation or ordinary posts, promise Neonrest endorsement, open pop-ups, autoplay media or run sponsor-provided scripts. Staff may pause or decline a campaign. Sponsorship does not buy moderation exceptions or control over community posts. Approval of a campaign is not an endorsement of the advertiser.

Measurement and privacy

Neonrest records direct-ad selections and clicks without attaching an account identifier or advertising cookie. A selection count is not proof that an ad was visible on screen or viewed by a person. Any sponsor report must use coarse totals and omit small groups with fewer than 20 selections or clicks; no individual event logs or account-linked reports are provided.

Images for direct campaigns are hosted by Neonrest. Clicking an ad opens the advertiser's site, which has its own privacy practices. Links use a no-referrer policy. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Google AdSense

Google site review and provider setup are separate from direct sponsorships. Publishing an ads.txt authorization record does not enable Google ad scripts or mean that Google has approved this site. Provider advertising requires a separate verified rollout with appropriate notices and choices.

Report an advertisement

Email ToS@neonrest.com with the ad title, destination and what concerned you. Do not include passwords or verification codes.
