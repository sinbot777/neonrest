How to read these numbers

  • Views are page loads, not unique people or visits. Refreshes count again. Known bots and site staff are excluded, but automated traffic can still get through.
  • Signup submissions are attempts, not individual people. A retry counts again. Queue acceptance does not prove an email reached an inbox. Verifications may belong to registrations from an earlier period; these are not a conversion funnel.
  • Only broad categories are counted. No profile handles, Circle or Vibe names, post IDs, query strings, search terms, IP addresses, raw referrers, device fingerprints, account IDs or content are saved by this suite.
  • No analytics cookies or third-party analytics requests are added. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control requests are excluded. Private account, security, support and management pages are not measured.
  • Daily aggregate counters retain the current UTC day and the previous 89 days. Older counters are pruned on the next measured request. Site statistics are separate from security logs and permanent account erasure.
  • Sandbox and Production collect separately. Measurements start when deployed; historical visitor activity cannot be reconstructed. Optional counter writes can be skipped if storage is busy, so totals are approximate.
  • For AI assistance, share the aggregate CSV and this explanation. Never provide account records, credentials, private content or raw access logs.
