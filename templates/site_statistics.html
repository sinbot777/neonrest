  • Page views are loads, not unique people or sessions. Refreshes count again. Known bots are excluded, but some automated traffic may still count.
  • Old daily counters are preserved. They cannot be split into staff, guests, members, sources, devices, communities or hours after the fact. Guest-only and member-only views use new detail exclusively.
  • No IP addresses, account identifiers, profile handles, post IDs, content, raw URLs, referrers, search terms or fingerprints are stored. Only public community IDs are recorded for named destination counts. Hidden or archived destinations lose their name and link in reports and exports.
  • No analytics cookies or third-party analytics requests. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control requests remain excluded, including staff traffic.
  • Account security, management, support and other sensitive pages are not measured. Sandbox and Production remain separate.
  • Each breakdown is independent: you cannot combine browser, destination, source and hour to trace a particular visitor. Daily/hourly aggregates retain 90 UTC days and are pruned on the next measured request. Busy or unavailable storage skips optional writes without interrupting the site.
  • For AI assistance, share the filtered aggregate CSV and these definitions. Do not share credentials, account records, private content or raw access logs.
