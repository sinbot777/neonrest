  • Page views are loads, not unique people or sessions. Refreshes count again. Known bots are excluded, but some automated traffic may still count.
  • Old daily counters are preserved. They cannot be split into staff, guests, members, sources, devices, communities or hours after the fact. Guest-only and member-only views use new detail exclusively.
  • No IP addresses, account identifiers, profile handles, post IDs, content, raw URLs, on-site search terms or fingerprints are stored. Imported provider search-query reports are separate aggregates. Optional browser/session tokens are stored only as keyed hashes. Only public community IDs are recorded for named destination counts. Hidden or archived destinations lose their name and link in reports and exports.
  • Optional first-party visitor/session cookies require Allow; no third-party analytics requests. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control requests remain excluded, including staff traffic.
  • Account security, management, support and other sensitive pages are not measured. Sandbox and Production remain separate.
  • Browser, device and destination breakdowns remain independent. Opted-in session funnels combine coarse entry/source categories and signup flags without account identifiers or page-by-page histories. Daily/hourly aggregates retain 90 UTC days and are pruned on the next measured request. Busy or unavailable storage skips optional writes without interrupting the site.
  • For AI assistance, share the filtered aggregate CSV and these definitions. Do not share credentials, account records, private content or raw access logs.
