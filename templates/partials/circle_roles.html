What Circle roles can do
Members
Read and share posts, reply, react, report concerns, choose new-post notifications and leave the Circle. Existing account and age safeguards apply.
Moderators
Member abilities, plus invite people, review join requests, remove ordinary members with a reason, pin a welcome post and review eligible Circle reports, approve content, remove it from the Circle or restore it. They cannot change Circle settings, manage other moderators, transfer ownership, archive the Circle or access private site cases.
Owner
Moderator abilities, plus edit Circle details and rules, appoint or remove moderators, transfer ownership and archive or restore the Circle. Ownership grants no extra access to private account data or site staff tools. The owner must transfer ownership before leaving an active Circle.
