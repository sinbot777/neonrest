Who can do what?

Site owner and admins
Archive and restore Vibes, assign or remove Vibe Managers, edit details and appearance, and curate posts.
Site moderators
Curate posts across Vibes and use authorized site report tools. Moderator status alone does not grant theme editing, archive, or role-assignment controls.
Assigned Vibe Managers
Edit the welcome message, rules and appearance, and remove or restore posts within assigned Vibes. This does not grant access to private site reports or otherwise inaccessible posts.
Site manager role
Keeps its existing site moderation and appearance permissions. Archive and Vibe Manager assignment remain owner/admin actions.
