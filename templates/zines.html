Home printer instructions

  1. Select the same paper size as your PDF and print at actual size (100%).
  2. For booklet PDFs, use landscape and duplex, flip on short edge. Use one PDF page per printed side; the file is already two-up. Do not enable the printer's booklet or multiple-pages mode.
  3. First print one sheet to check front/back orientation and your printer's paper feed. If printing manually, use your printer's duplex instructions.
  4. Keep the outermost sheet outside, nest the remaining sheets, fold in half and staple along the spine.

Standard PDFs are portrait pages in normal reading order. Their duplex setting is normally flip on long edge. Printer drivers vary; a physical first-sheet test is recommended.
