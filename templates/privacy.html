Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Neonrest is operated by Brenden Kelly, an individual based in Oregon, United States (“Neonrest,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share personal information when you use Neonrest’s website, features, and services (the “Service”).

Our approach is to collect information needed to operate the Service, protect its community, and support its financial sustainability while limiting unnecessary collection and disclosure.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you register or manage your account, we collect information you provide, including:

  • Actual name: Required during signup for account administration. Providing your name does not, by itself, mean it is publicly displayed.
  • Email address: Used for account access, verification, recovery, service communications, and notifications according to available settings.
  • Handle and codename: Used to identify your account publicly and support mentions, discovery, and social features.
  • Date of birth: Used to determine eligibility and apply age-based protections. Your date of birth is not displayed publicly.
  • Authentication information: Used to secure your account, including password-verification information and information needed for any security features you enable.
  • Agreement records: For new Sandbox signups from October 13, 2026, the accepted Terms version, acknowledged Privacy Policy version and acceptance time. Older accounts may have no such record; we do not infer acceptance from an account’s existence. Family consent and review records are separate.

Acceptance of the Terms does not constitute blanket consent to optional advertising or tracking.

Profile information and content

We collect information you choose to voluntarily provide, such as pronouns, biography, avatar, interests, profile customization, posts, comments, uploaded files, and other content.

We also process social interactions, such as friend requests, connections, follows, reactions, and audience selections.

Whether information is visible to others depends on the feature, your settings, and applicable age-based restrictions.

Profile location is optional information you enter yourself and may be visible according to your profile settings. A geographic proximity feed and creative/business pages are planned features. Circles support public and members-only posts; {% if messaging_enabled|default(false) %}Private text messaging is available to eligible participants. Messages, conversation membership, read position, notification preferences and message settings are stored on Neonrest, not end-to-end encrypted. Presence is invisible by default; other statuses and an optional away message are shared only with eligible friends while the Messages page is active. No last-seen history or read receipts are shared. Conversation pages and message content are excluded from analytics. Participants can submit a selected message and an explanation to authorized staff; the reported message is preserved in the case system, without automatically opening the rest of the conversation. Account exports include the sender's own messages. When permanent erasure is activated and preservation checks permit it, account erasure removes the account's sent messages and membership links; other participants retain their own messages. Existing backups and restricted moderation evidence follow the retention rules below.{% else %}direct messaging remains planned.{% endif %}

Communications and safety information

We collect information submitted through support requests, reports, appeals, rights complaints, and other communications with us.

We may retain relevant content and activity records to investigate abuse, enforce our rules, protect accounts, or resolve disputes.

Technical information

When you access the Service, our systems and hosting providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, and error or security logs.

We use this information for service delivery, troubleshooting, security, abuse prevention, and limited operational measurement.

Network security and abuse prevention

For account security, abuse investigation and temporary network restrictions, we record validated client IP addresses, recorded account IDs when signed in, request methods, response status, redacted route/path, event type, counts and first/last times. We record authentication requests and errors, rate limits and suspicious scans; we do not record passwords, recovery or authenticator codes, session cookies, request bodies or query strings. These private security records are separate from optional visitor analytics and operate independently of analytics consent, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. They retain up to 30 days, subject to bounded capacity and any documented legal preservation obligation. Owner/admin access requires recent authentication and 2FA, and log access and restriction changes are audited. Restrictions expire within seven days unless separately reviewed and renewed; restriction records and their reasons are removed within 30 days after expiry. No IPs or account IDs are included in the statistics dashboard or CSV. Sanitized IP-free error-path summaries retain up to 30 days and honor DNT/GPC. Provider security/access logs and backups have separate retention.

Security logging description updated October 18, 2026.

Aggregate site statistics

We count page loads, hourly activity, broad navigation pairs and signup or email-verification outcomes. Separate guest, member and staff totals help exclude maintenance activity. We derive broad referral, browser and device categories without retaining raw referring URLs or browser strings; sanitized public referring hostnames are counted. Public community IDs support aggregate Vibe and public Circle counts; current public status is checked before names or links appear in reports. Profiles, private posts and hidden communities are not individually identified. The suite stores no IP addresses, account identifiers, content, raw URLs, on-site search terms or fingerprints. Optional visitor/session cookies require Allow; no third-party analytics requests are made. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control override Allow. Aggregate counters retain up to 90 UTC days, with older counters removed on the next measured request. Hosting and security logs are separate. Statistics description updated October 18, 2026.

Discovery and reliability reports combine coarse referral/landing categories and signup-stage flags within opted-in sessions, aggregate feature HTTP response classes, and bucketed server-processing times for safe route templates. No private content, account IDs or additional browser identifiers are collected. Search-discovery reports may separately contain aggregate search phrases, clicks, impressions and average positions exported from Google Search Console or Bing Webmaster Tools. These provider reports are not linked to Neonrest accounts, IPs or browser sessions; provider privacy filters and reporting timezones apply. Reports retain at most 90 days, with bounded row limits. No Google Analytics or other third-party visitor-tracking script is added by these reports.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create, maintain, and secure accounts.
  • Provide profiles, posts, social features, and personalization you request.
  • Apply eligibility requirements and age-based visibility and contact restrictions.
  • Maintain existing family links and related consent and safety records where applicable.
  • Deliver account messages and notifications.
  • Investigate reports, moderate content, and enforce our Terms.
  • Diagnose problems and improve reliability and accessibility.
  • Display advertising and measure its performance as described below.
  • Respond to privacy requests, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

We do not use private communications, actual names, email addresses, or exact dates of birth to select advertisements.

3. What Other Users Can See

Your handle, codename, and information you choose to place on your profile may be visible to other users, subject to the Service’s settings and age-based restrictions.

Posts, comments, reactions, and other activity may be visible to their intended audiences. Public content may also be accessible to visitors or search engines where the Service permits public access.

Signed-in users under 18 can read ordinary posts and replies only from people within three years of their age or their approved linked parents. Marking a post public does not remove this limit for a signed-in younger reader. Adult public posts remain available to visitors who are not signed in. Explicit public staff announcements are a separate exception; the author’s other posts and unrelated adult replies remain restricted. Private audiences, blocks, moderation and account restrictions still apply.

The same age window limits signed-in younger users’ access to ordinary profiles, friend listings and profile files. Approved linked parents remain accessible. Out-of-window administrators, managers and moderators have a limited staff profile showing their handle, role, avatar and reporting/support options; this does not expose personal profile details, connections, music or ordinary activity. Existing privacy settings, blocks and account restrictions still apply.

Your email address and date of birth are not displayed publicly by default. Your actual name is collected privately for account administration and is not displayed on public profiles. Collecting a name does not verify your identity. Older accounts may not yet have supplied a name.

Authorized moderators, managers and administrators can view users and posts outside ordinary audience and age restrictions for moderation and safety purposes. This does not make that content public. Approved linked-parent relationships can also create exceptions to age-based visibility and contact restrictions; ordinary audience and other access rules still apply.

Other people may copy, capture, or redistribute information they can access. Audience restrictions cannot prevent every form of copying or disclosure.

4. Advertising and Sponsorships

Neonrest supports staff-managed direct sponsorships to help cover operating costs. Eligible campaigns can appear as labeled cards or a lower banner. Google AdSense review and activation are separate; an ads.txt record does not load third-party advertising scripts. Read our advertising and sponsor standards.

How advertisements are selected

Our default approach is contextual advertising: advertisements may be selected based on the page or Vibe being viewed, campaign schedules, and other information about the placement rather than a profile of your activity across unrelated websites.

We may use age information internally to exclude advertisements that are inappropriate for an age group without disclosing your date of birth to advertisers.

We do not use sensitive profile information, private communications, or activity across unrelated websites to build advertising profiles.

What advertisers receive

We may provide advertisers with aggregate reports, such as the number of times an advertisement was displayed or clicked and the pages or Vibes where it appeared.

We do not provide advertisers with your actual name, email address, date of birth, private content, or a list connecting individual accounts to advertisement views or clicks.

We take reasonable steps to avoid reports that identify individual users, including through unusually small audience groups.

Advertiser websites and external resources

If you click an advertisement, you will leave Neonrest or connect to the advertiser’s service. That service may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and referral information, subject to your browser settings and its own privacy practices.

Similarly, content loaded directly from an external provider can disclose technical information to that provider. We aim to host advertising assets ourselves where feasible and limit unnecessary third-party requests.

Changes to advertising practices

This policy does not authorize unrestricted sharing with advertising networks.

Before introducing third-party behavioral advertising or materially different advertising disclosures, we will update this policy, provide required notices and choices, and obtain consent where required by law.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies or similar technologies where needed to maintain login sessions, protect accounts, remember settings, and provide requested features.

Basic advertising measurement may record advertisement displays and clicks without tracking users across unrelated websites.

Optional third-party behavioral advertising trackers are not part of the advertising approach described in this policy. If we introduce optional technologies requiring consent or opt-out controls, we will provide the applicable notices and choices before using them.

You can manage cookies through your browser. Disabling essential cookies may prevent login or other features from working.

6. When We Share Information

We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

  • At your direction: When you publish content, interact with others, or use a feature that requires sharing.
  • With service providers: Providers supporting hosting, storage, email delivery, security, or other operational functions may process information needed to perform those services. Neonrest is hosted on PythonAnywhere, which may process site data and technical logs to provide hosting.
  • For moderation and support: Authorized personnel may access information reasonably necessary to investigate issues, respond to requests, and protect the community.
  • For legal and safety reasons: When reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, or address security incidents.
  • For rights complaints: Relevant complaint information, including identity and contact details, may be shared with the affected user where necessary to evaluate or respond to the claim.
  • During an ownership transition: Information may be transferred as part of a sale, reorganization, or transfer of the Service, subject to applicable law and required notice. Any materially different use remains subject to applicable notice and consent requirements.

We limit disclosures to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

7. Children and Teenagers

Public signup is limited to people ages 13 and older, subject to any higher minimum age required by applicable law. New enrollment for children under 13, including parent-invited enrollment, is not available.

We use date-of-birth information to apply age-based protections for users under 18. These may limit discovery, visibility, connections, and other interactions between age groups. Approved existing family relationships may provide exceptions to age-based restrictions, as described in Section 3.

We do not use personal information of users we know are under 18 for behavioral advertising or disclose it to advertising networks for that purpose.

For existing family-linked accounts, we may maintain the parent and child account relationship, contact permissions, and records of consent and its review. Where a signed consent letter has been submitted, it may contain the parent's name, signature, date, and parent and child handles. Review records may include the verification method, reference, reviewer, review time, and consent status. Submitted letters are restricted to the submitting parent and authorized reviewing staff through the consent workflow.

Where applicable, a parent or legal guardian may request review or deletion of their child's personal information or withdraw consent by contacting ToS@neonrest.com. Existing Family Settings controls may also allow approved parents to manage permitted peer contacts and revoke consent. We verify parental authority before providing private information or acting on a request.

Sandbox calculates birthdays using the account’s saved birthday timezone. It is confirmed once and kept separate from the display timezone. Accounts without a saved timezone use UTC until confirmation in Age and safety. Posts and replies made while under 18 keep their age protection after the author turns 18; parent-specific access ends when the child turns 18.

Existing family-linked parents can submit and track data-review or deletion requests in Family Settings. Staff must review current guardian authority before approving a private export, available for 24 hours, or scheduling deletion. Consent withdrawal clears parent contact grants and restricts under-13 access when no other approved parent remains. A parent-directed deletion request cannot be cancelled by a minor without reviewed guardian or staff authorization. Ordinary DOB changes remain unavailable; Age and safety records requests for review without changing the date.

If we learn that someone under 13 has registered through public signup, we will restrict access and address the collected information as required by applicable law. This policy does not establish an exemption from applicable children's privacy requirements.

8. How Long We Keep Information

Account and profile information is kept while you use Neonrest. Deactivation hides your profile and posts but keeps your information for reactivation.

In Sandbox, an account deletion request hides your profile and posts. You can cancel it through Account data during a 30-day waiting period. Permanent account erasure and the combined retention cleanup job are currently disabled in Sandbox and Production; eligible requests remain queued for review. Contact ToS@neonrest.com for help or a parent-directed deletion request.

The periods below are retention and removal-eligibility targets. They do not mean that automatic erasure is active. Staff review document removal and backup rotation; unresolved issues, preservation holds and documented obligations can delay removal. Holds require review and do not expire merely because a review date passes.

Documents submitted for a privacy request

When necessary to verify a request, staff may invite you to submit a limited, redacted identity or authority document through a private link. No account is required. Each invitation accepts one PDF, PNG or JPEG up to 5 MiB and expires after 24 hours. Do not send identity documents by ordinary email. We use the evidence to assess that request; submission does not automatically establish authority or permit disclosure.

New request documents are stored encrypted outside public files and restricted to authorized staff with recent authentication. They become eligible for staff-confirmed removal 30 days after document review and final request/appeal resolution; outstanding issues and holds postpone eligibility. We retain minimal review results and references with the request record. These new submissions are excluded from routine source/database backup exports. Legacy signed family-consent evidence follows its separate reviewed schedule below.

  • Account deletion: After the 30-day cancellation period, remove the account profile, authentication information, relationships, owned posts, replies, edit history, and associated uploads. Replies from other people in a deleted post's thread are also removed; their contributions elsewhere remain. Shared theme contributions can remain without the creator account link.
  • Unused registrations and friend requests: Remove unverified registrations and unanswered friend requests after 30 days. Operational staff, reserved helpers, and accounts currently disabled by staff require separate review.
  • Sign-in records: Remove verification, recovery and email-change tokens seven days after expiry. Remove expired sessions seven days after expiry and revoked sessions seven days after their last activity. Rate-limit counters expire after one day without activity; valid tokens and current limits remain effective.
  • Queued account emails: Encrypt message contents and recipients while waiting to deliver verification, recovery and account-security messages. Pending payloads expire within one hour and are cleared after completion, cancellation or the next successful expiry-cleanup run. Keep minimal job status for up to 30 days after expiry and a separate address-free attempt record for 45 days after expiry to prevent restored backups from repeating sends. Rotate the separate mail encryption key every 30 days.
  • Service records: Keep application error and delivery records for 30 days, abuse, security and account email-change event records for 90 days, notifications for 90 days, and saved authorization form drafts for one day. Remove unreferenced avatar and media files after 30 days.
  • Consent letters: Keep letters associated with an approved family relationship while needed to document that relationship. Remove letters not associated with an approved relationship after 30 days from submission, or when the linked account is erased. Parent or child deletion removes the associated family relationship and its contact permissions.
  • Family data and DOB-review requests: Closed, declined and completed access-review records have a 90-day retention target after the final request or appeal resolution. Unremoved verification evidence prevents cleanup of its associated request record. Pending reviews and scheduled deletion requests require resolution before cleanup; they are not removed merely because they are old.
  • Managed Sandbox backups: Managed backups made through Site operations have a 30-day retention target, with a verified restore point retained and preservation exceptions. Rotation remains an operator task while the combined job is disabled. Previously created archives and hosting-provider logs or backups are outside this routine schedule and require separate review; their deletion periods have not yet been verified.

Restricted reports, appeals, staff action records, and preserved moderation evidence may remain after account deletion for safety, dispute handling, and applicable legal obligations. Closed moderation cases are marked for retention review after one year. Review does not automatically erase protected evidence; staff must decide whether continued retention has a specific need. No fixed automatic deletion deadline is currently enforced for those records.

Deletion from the working service does not immediately remove information from existing backups or copies that other people have saved. Managed backup rotation follows the target and preservation exceptions above; it is not currently automatic. Restores stay offline until current preservation/deletion decisions and private-file inventory are reconciled and restored invitations, sessions and export/recovery grants are invalidated. We do not keep children's information solely because it might be useful in the future; parent requests and consent withdrawal receive separate review.

9. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on available features and applicable law, you may:

  • Edit supported account and profile information. Date of birth is locked after signup and cannot be changed through ordinary settings. To report an incorrect date, use Account → Age and safety or contact ToS@neonrest.com with your handle and a brief explanation. Do not email identity documents or your full date of birth. For children, the linked parent or guardian should contact us. A request does not change the stored date or relax age protections.   • Change your sign-in email under Account safety. The current address stays active until you confirm the new one. Completion signs out every device, invalidates outstanding recovery links and notifies the old address. Your password, date of birth, profile, friendships and two-factor authentication stay in place.
  • Change audience and notification settings.
  • Delete content.
  • Use available account controls or contact us to request account deletion. Account deactivation alone does not necessarily delete information.
  • Request access to, correction of, a copy of, or deletion of your personal information.
  • Exercise applicable rights to restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact ToS@neonrest.com to submit a privacy request or get help with these choices.

We may need to verify your identity or authority. We will respond within applicable legal deadlines and explain any lawful reason we cannot fully fulfill a request.

Where applicable law provides a right to appeal a denied request, you may appeal by replying to our decision or emailing us with the subject “Privacy Appeal.”

We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Deletion does not remove copies independently saved or redistributed by other users or records held by third parties under their own policies.
