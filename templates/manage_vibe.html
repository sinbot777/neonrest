How to use these tools

Choose the smallest action that addresses the issue. Removing a post here removes its association with this Vibe; the original post and its other Vibes remain. Restore it from Removed posts. Private and site-hidden content is not disclosed to Vibe Managers.

Archiving hides a Vibe from discovery and stops new posts being added. Existing posts remain readable under their usual permissions. Authors can still edit posts already in it or remove the archived tag. Restore the Vibe to reopen posting.

Every change needs a factual reason. Do not put private identity documents, authentication codes, or unrelated personal details in a reason. If using AI assistance, supply only information you are authorized to share; review its suggested action and wording yourself. Nothing is submitted until you click the action button.

Use site reports for safety issues requiring site staff. A Vibe Manager cannot view reports sent privately to site staff. Changes to staff-authored content require the site owner.
