Retention and restore rules

Raw submissions: eligible 30 days after document review and final request/appeal resolution, with holds and later preservation decisions extending eligibility. Staff must review the due queue regularly. Request and review records: 90 days after final resolution, subject to preservation. Managed rolling database backups: 30-day retention target, subject to holds and a verified restore point; raw identity files are excluded from routine backup exports. Document removal is staff-confirmed, and backup rotation remains an operator task while the combined retention job is disabled. Do not remove held evidence or legacy archives merely to meet a rotation date.

After a restore, keep the site offline, preserve the independent invitation receipts and separately secured document encryption key, revoke restored invitations and authentication grants, reapply post-backup preservation/deletion decisions, check raw-file inventory, and verify the restored state before reopening. Do not restore an older receipt ledger over the current one. Legacy signed family-consent evidence needs its own documented review; it is not silently assigned this new submission schedule.
