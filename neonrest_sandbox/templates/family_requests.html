We help parents and guardians protect young people and request their information safely. We check identity, authority and what may be shared while protecting everyone involved.

What evidence might help?

A driver's license, state ID or passport can help establish identity. A birth certificate or adoption record can help establish the relationship. Guardianship papers or relevant parts of a custody order may establish current authority or restrictions. These are examples, not a requirement to submit every document.

Contact ToS@neonrest.com first. We will explain what is needed and arrange private submission. Do not put identity documents, passwords, verification codes or full dates of birth in notes or ordinary email. You may redact unrelated details. Private communications require separate disclosure review.

Requests without a linked account and questions can be sent to ToS@neonrest.com. DOB changes require staff review.
