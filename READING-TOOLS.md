# T17 — Reading tools

Implemented October 21, 2026 (Pacific), separately in Production and Sandbox.

Feed views are All posts, Friends, Followed, Your Vibes, Your Circles and Bookmarks. A verified member can save a default at /account/feed. An explicit view temporarily overrides that default. Pagination retains the chosen view. All views remain chronological and filter through the existing post-visibility check before LIMIT. Followed rechecks current eligibility and revocation; Your Circles requires active membership; Your Vibes uses up to 50 selected active, promoted Vibes. All posts preserves discovery. Selecting a Vibe does not subscribe to notifications or grant access.

Bookmarks store a private reference, not a content copy. Current age, block, mute, moderation, post-audience and Circle checks apply when displaying or saving. Edits update the original preview; losing access hides it. /account/bookmarks offers bounded management, including generic unavailable entries without text or author details. Saving is idempotent; each account can keep up to 2,000 bookmarks. Forms use public post identifiers and CSRF. Owners can remove their own unavailable references.

Drafts are explicit private saves, separate from the older reauthorization-recovery draft. Save up to 50 drafts, each with up to 20,000 text characters and bounded composer metadata. Attachments and staff-announcement status are not saved; clear file selections before saving and reattach files when publishing. /drafts lists bounded pages, offers a private editor and resumes in the full composer. A stale Circle, label or original share never silently becomes a public post: current access is rechecked and the owner can explicitly repair the destination or remove a lost share reference through the private editor. Concurrent updates use revisions; publishing consumes a current owned draft inside the post transaction. Failed publishing retains the draft. Duplicate publishing cannot create a second post.

New routes require current verified sessions. Writes require CSRF, same-origin validation and bounded mutation rates. Private pages are uncached. Normal post publishing retains its existing interaction restrictions. New tables/indexes are additive; migration does not backfill preferences or rewrite existing users, posts, audiences, relationships, notifications or Circles. Existing muting and notification preferences remain in place.

Own exports include private reading settings, selected Vibe names, bookmarks as eligible public references and the owner's draft data. The new data is not silently added to reviewed guardian exports. Future erasure cleanup removes owned records and references to erased posts; preservation discovery checks affected bookmark owners. Permanent erasure remains disabled. No cleanup activation, invitations, follow notifications, SMTP sends, subscription notifications or Afterglow rollout is introduced.

Rollback restores source only. Keep additive tables and newer records; do not rewind a user database. Earlier code ignores these private reading preferences and cannot operate the newer draft UI until restored. Separate Production and Sandbox source/configuration remain separate. Remaining deep-offset/reaction growth work is independent of T17.

Validation: 119 focused checks per environment cover current access, ownership, explicit/default views, pagination, draft revisions/concurrency, duplicate publishing, failure preservation, limits, exports, related holds and future cleanup. Broader regression and hosted deployment results are recorded in the dev log and release evidence.
