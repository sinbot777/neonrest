# T31 Seller Studio — private shop and listing preparation

Scope: Page-linked private shop drafts and physical/digital listing drafts. No listing publication, paid business activation, checkout, payment, shipping label purchase, recurring membership or marketplace fee collection is enabled.

Eligible adult accounts may prepare shops attached to creative or business Pages; a paid business Page is not required. Only a Page owner may create the shop or change its name, description and policies. Current adult Page managers/editors may edit listing drafts. Existing account verification, disable/inactivity/posting restrictions, Page moderation, age/contact and block checks remain authoritative. Revoked delegates immediately lose access. Current Page ownership must match the shop's captured owner; transfers freeze the old shop for review rather than exposing its drafts to a new owner.

Shop and listing edits require CSRF and fresh authentication, with per-account write throttling. SQLite transactions recheck access and optimistic revisions before saving. A stale submission returns a conflict and copyable unsaved entries. Lists are bounded; initial limits are 100 retained listings per shop and ten variation rows per listing. Archived Pages retain read-only shop access; archived listing drafts can be restored. No permanent delete is provided.

Listings contain escaped plain-text title/description, physical/digital type, exact USD cents, base quantity, up to ten distinct combination labels with individual prices/quantities, delivery/processing terms, usage rights and return/refund terms. Variations sum to the listing quantity; a blank variation price uses the base price. Private saved-draft previews show these details and shop policies. This release is text-only: product images and downloadable product files await marketplace storage/delivery controls. Commissions and membership tiers remain later stages.

Additive seller_shops, seller_listings and seller_studio_events tables store drafts and minimal action history; they do not replace Page or billing records. Only draft/archived listing states exist, and publishing/checkout actions are rejected even if forged. Owner account JSON/ZIP exports include owned shop/listing data; exports do not confer ownership of another uploader's media. Private studio responses use no-store/noindex/no-referrer. Studio links are added to existing Page navigation.

Required verification: both actual source layouts on disposable copied databases; owner/delegate/cross-Page/guest/minor/revocation/block/restriction/transfer checks; exact decimal prices and variation bounds; escaped preview; fresh-auth/CSRF; stale edits, archive/restore and no commercial transitions; account export integration; existing Page/privacy/export/recovery regressions; additive migration/backup restoration preserving old table fingerprints; desktop/mobile keyboard/render checks and source-verified rollout. Live fixture shops are forbidden.
