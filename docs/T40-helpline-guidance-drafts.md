# HelpLine onboarding guidance — published October 22, 2026

Published as ordinary public Fresh Starts posts by the owner-authorized maintenance batch. These posts retain current audience and age restrictions; the shared User guide remains the onboarding source for younger members. No staff role or announcement bypass was added to HelpLine.

## 1. Welcome to Neonrest

Welcome! Start with your profile: choose a handle, optional codename, avatar, and the details you want to share. Your actual name and date of birth stay private. Optional public profile fields are visible wherever your profile is available, so leave anything blank that you would rather keep to yourself. The User guide is linked at the bottom of every page.

## 2. Your welcome friend and Top 9

You may start with @brendoom as a welcome friend and in Top 9. You can rearrange Top 9, remove the entry, or remove the friendship from Friends. It won't be added back just because you visit again. Friendship doesn't bypass age or privacy rules; younger members may see a limited staff support profile rather than someone's personal content.

## 3. Vibes, Circles and audiences

Vibes connect conversations by interest or feeling. Fresh Starts is a good first stop. New Vibes normally open after three distinct people tag posts with the same name. Circles are separate communities with membership and their own rules. Before posting, check the audience. Tagging a Vibe doesn't publish a private post, and joining a Circle doesn't grant access to every person's content. Never share someone else's private content without permission.

## 4. Find your pace and get help

Feed preferences let you choose a chronological view and save a default. Bookmark a post to find it again, or save a private text draft before publishing. Drafts don't save file attachments. Use a post's options menu to report a concern, and use Account safety for password and session controls. Contact ToS@neonrest.com for help; don't send passwords, authenticator codes, recovery codes or identity documents by email.
